# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Sureflow a welcoming, respectful,
and constructive experience for everyone.

We value technical disagreement when it is conducted in good faith. Contributors
should be able to challenge designs, implementation decisions, and assumptions
without personal attacks or harassment.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and technical decisions.
- Giving and receiving feedback in good faith.
- Clearly identifying uncertainty or disagreement.
- Respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Prioritizing project safety, correctness, maintainability, and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or abusive conduct.
- Malicious behavior toward contributors, users, or project infrastructure.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, documentation, dependencies, or
  configuration.

## Technical Disagreement

Sureflow is an engineering project. Strong technical disagreement is allowed
and can be valuable.

When disagreements occur:

1. State the technical claim clearly.
2. Provide evidence where possible.
3. Distinguish facts, assumptions, and preferences.
4. Explain trade-offs.
5. Prefer reproducible experiments or benchmarks when practical.
6. Avoid turning technical disagreement into personal conflict.

No contributor is expected to agree merely to maintain consensus.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for maintaining a constructive project environment.

They may:

- Moderate discussions.
- Request evidence or clarification.
- Close discussions that have become abusive or unproductive.
- Reject contributions that create security, legal, or project-integrity risks.
- Temporarily restrict participation when necessary to protect the project.

Technical decisions should remain as transparent and evidence-based as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and activities where a person
is representing or participating in the Sureflow project.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers through the contact method published in the repository.

For security vulnerabilities, do **not** use public issue trackers. Follow the
process in `SECURITY.md`.

Reports should include enough information to understand what happened while
avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and, where appropriate, privately.

Possible responses include:

- clarification or warning;
- request to stop the behavior;
- removal of inappropriate content;
- temporary participation restrictions;
- permanent participation restrictions for serious or repeated violations.

The response will consider the severity, context, and recurrence of the behavior.
